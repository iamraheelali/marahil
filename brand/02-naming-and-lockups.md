# Naming, trademark, and domain

## Hierarchy

| Level | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| House | MARAHIL | مراحل |
| Legal | MARAHIL by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics | مراحل من عطور ومستحضرات تجميل ماريا رحيل خان |
| Spoken | The House of MARAHIL | بيت مراحل |
| Juice | Chapter name only (Bad’, Ishraq, Wasl, Layl, Athar, Maria) | بدء، إشراق، وصل، ليل، أثر، ماريا |

Never put the house name and the juice name in the same role.  
**Wrong:** “Marahil Eau de Parfum 100ml.”  
**Right:** “Ishraq — MARAHIL.”

---

## Why this matters

Two collisions already exist:

1. **`marahil.com`** is taken. Historic use: Saudi maternity retailer *مراحل الأمومة* (Marahil 4 Maternity). Do not build the house on this URL.
2. **Ard Al Zaafaran (Dubai)** already sells a mass-market EDP titled **Marahil** (typical notes: lemon / oud / saffron / musk). Same word, same category, same country. This is the serious risk — Google, Noon, and Amazon will mix the two.

**SKU rule:** no product whose only commercial name is “Marahil.” The house is MARAHIL; juices are chapters.

---

## Trademark (UAE, before public launch)

File at the Ministry of Economy:

- Word mark **MARAHIL** / **مراحل**
- Lockup **MARAHIL by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics**
- Later: the seal device (Direction B)

**Classes:** Nice **3** (perfumes, cosmetics) and **35** (retail / online).  
Commission a clearance search that explicitly includes Ard Al Zaafaran’s MARAHIL.  
If filing from outside the UAE, use a registered local agent.

## Domains

Checked 4 Sep 2026 via RDAP (404 = likely unregistered). Confirm and buy at a registrar before announcing.

| Domain | Role |
|---|---|
| **marahilparfums.com** | Primary commerce (recommended) |
| **thehouseofmarahil.com** | Alternate / redirect |
| **mariaraheelkhan.com** | Founder / press |
| **marahil.ae** | UAE signal if eligible (local presence / agent often required) |
| marahilperfumes.com, marahilparfum.com | Defensive |

**Do not chase `marahil.com`** unless counsel says acquisition is clean and cheap. Maternity history plus the Ard Al Zaafaran SKU make it a poor brand URL.

**Taken / avoid as primary:** marahil.com, marahil.co (appears registered).

**Email:** `studio@`, `orders@`, `press@` on the primary domain.
