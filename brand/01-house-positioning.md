# House positioning

**The House of MARAHIL**  
MARAHIL (مراحل) by Maria Raheel Khan  
Founded in Abu Dhabi, United Arab Emirates.

## Our Story

Every stage has a scent. MARAHIL means stages in Arabic — the quiet turns we live through.

Maria Raheel Khan founded MARAHIL in Abu Dhabi. The house joins French haute perfumery — Eau de Parfum, Parfum, Extrait — with Gulf heritage: Bakhoor, Oils, Creams, and Scented Candles.

Haute Parfum is made in France. Everyday editions, Beauty, and Jewellery are assembled in the UAE, from the same juice.

Arabic and English are written as originals. They share the bottle, the box, and the card. Neither waits as a translation.

---

## Meaning

*مراحل* means stages — the quiet turns a life takes.  
We do not launch a new floral because summer arrived. We name a *marhala*: a stage you can wear.

The chapters stay: Bad’, Ishraq, Wasl, Layl, Athar, and Maria.

One sentence: **Every stage has a scent.**

## Audience

- Women and men in the UAE and GCC who already buy niche or Gulf houses, not only designer counters  
- Bilingual shoppers who expect Arabic to be equal, not a Google-translated footer  
- Gift buyers (Ramadan, weddings, Eid) who want a named house, not a grey-market bottle  
- Diaspora and visitors who want something that feels Dubai/Abu Dhabi without looking like a souvenir oud

**Not for:** replica shoppers, clone-catalogue hunters, “inspired by” language.

---

## Tone

**Voice:** calm, precise, slightly literary. Short sentences. Arabic and English written as originals, not translations of each other.

**Do say:** the house, the *marhala*, original juice, atelier, discovery, ritual.  
**Do not say:** luxury lifestyle, olfactory journey (overused), unisex bomb, best Arabic perfume online, 100% original guaranteed (sounds like a fake shop).

Authenticity is shown by IFRA, ingredients, and the founder’s name — not by shouting “original.”

---

## Promise

Every MARAHIL fragrance is a stage. The bottle silhouette does not change. The collar color and the juice name do. You collect chapters, not random SKUs.
