# Beauty and other products

Perfumes first. Beauty is a **ritual around the juice**, not a second brand.

Follow Initio (hair perfume + body cream) and Mahra M1 (hair/body from day one), not a full makeup counter.

---

## With perfume launch (beauty Phase 1)

Only two juices get rituals at first: **Athar** and **Maria**.

| Product | Size (indicative) | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Hair mist | 50ml | Gulf heat; extraits on hair are a real use case |
| Body oil or cream | 150–200ml | Completes the “ritual” add-on without a new scent |

Plus **The First Marahil** discovery set.

Do not launch lip, powder, or candles in the same week as the five EDPs.

---

## Beauty Phase 2 (after the juice is real and selling)

- Concentrated **attar** of Wasl or Ishraq (oil, small bottle, Gulf-native format)
- **Bakhoor / home** for Layl (one SKU, not a home line)
- Hand cream in Athar (gift-with-purchase or small paid SKU)

---

## Beauty Phase 3 (only if Phase 1 sells)

- Lip oil in Athar (Kayali-adjacent, quieter)
- Candle in Layl or Ishraq
- Shimmer / body veil only if it matches the kit (ivory/pewter), never festival glitter

**Still do not:** a full color-cosmetics line, unisex body mist in ten flavours, or “inspired by” dupes of other houses.

The expanded boxed catalog (oil, cream, lotion, attar, bakhoor, candle, earrings, necklace, rings) with wear profiles and AED bands lives in [08-product-catalog.md](08-product-catalog.md). Jewelry stays pewter / white gold to match the seal — not a second jewellery brand.

---

## Naming

Ritual products carry the juice name, then the format, then the house:

- `Athar Hair Mist — MARAHIL`
- `Maria Body Oil — MARAHIL`

Arabic on pack as equals, not as a subtitle afterthought.
