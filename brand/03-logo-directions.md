# Logo directions

Three directions were explored. **Ship A + B.** C is a supporting signature, not a competing logo.

---

## Direction A — Dual-script house mark (primary)

Latin `MARAHIL` stacked over or beside Arabic `مراحل`, founder line in small caps underneath.

**Why it wins:** Quiet Gulf-house language (Amouage pattern), bilingual from the first second, works on a bottle, a box, and a site header.

**Correct hierarchy**

1. `MARAHIL`
2. `مراحل`
3. `by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics`

`PERFUMES AND COSMETICS` is the founder-line category, not a co-equal wordmark. If a concept image shows a giant `M` or `PERFUMES` at house-name size, treat that as noise — do not lock it.

## Direction B — Stage seal (secondary)

Circular pewter coin. Five arcs = the five *marahil* (stages). Center: `MRK`. Optional outer ring: `MARAHIL` or `مراحل`. Hairline of stage red on the inner ring only.

**Why it exists:** Avatars, wax, magnetic cap, favicon. A wordmark cannot survive at 32 px; a seal can.

**Best concept frames**

- Full seal: [visuals/marahil-seal-mrk.png](visuals/marahil-seal-mrk.png)
- Cap / favicon: [visuals/marahil-seal-favicon.png](visuals/marahil-seal-favicon.png)

Do not use the seal as the website header. It is a stamp, not a title.

---

## Direction C — Founder intimate (supporting)

`MARAHIL` as the product brand; `Maria Raheel Khan` as a written signature underneath (Kayali / Mona pattern).

**Where it lives:** press kit, inner leaflet, “The House” page, box interior.  
**Where it does not live:** app icon, bottle cap, paid ads at small size.

Concept: [visuals/marahil-direction-c-founder.png](visuals/marahil-direction-c-founder.png)

---

## Decision

| Role | Direction |
|---|---|
| Primary logo | A — dual-script wordmark |
| Secondary mark | B — MRK stage seal |
| Human line | C — founder signature |
| Color | Ink / ivory / pewter / stage red (see [00-brand-kit.md](00-brand-kit.md)) |
