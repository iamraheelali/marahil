# MARAHIL Brand Kit

**House:** MARAHIL  
**Arabic:** مراحل  
**Legal lockup:** MARAHIL by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics  
**Status:** Concept kit (Phase 1). Not production vectors yet.

Primary system: **gold circular house crest** (Arabic `مراحل` + Latin `MARAHIL` in a filigree ring) plus a dual-script wordmark. Direction C (founder signature) remains a supporting line for press.

### Master hardware

One cap / lid / box medallion for the whole house. Production traces this frame:

- Master crest: [visuals/marahil-crest.jpg](visuals/marahil-crest.jpg)  
  Circular gold-foil mark on night black. Ornate arabesque filigree ring. Arabic `مراحل` over tracked serif `MARAHIL`. Thin gold rule.

**Same crest on:** bottle cap top, box lid, cream lid, candle lid, attar cap, discovery vial caps, jewellery discs.  
**Same wordmark beside it:** header and press — `MARAHIL` over `مراحل`.

Juice name (ISHRAQ, MARIA, ATHAR…) lives only on the **plaque or side print**, never inside the crest.  
Cap metal is **matte black**; the gold lives in the crest, not as a yellow-gold bottle body.

## Palette

Four house colours stay the core. Phase 2 chrome uses **night + gold crest** so the mark reads at header size. Jewellery may offer silver finish and champagne gold finish. Bottle *bodies* stay glass; caps stay matte black with the gold crest, not a yellow-gold shell.

**Rules**
- House mark is the gold circular crest. Do not invent a second logo.
- Juice color lives on the **bottle collar**, not in the logo.
- No gradients on the crest.

---

## Typography

| Role | Spec | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Latin display | Playfair Display | High-contrast serif. `MARAHIL` tracked. |
| Arabic display | Noto Naskh Arabic | `مراحل`. Never squash Arabic to match Latin width. |
| Founder line | IBM Plex Sans, letterspaced | `by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics` |
| UI | IBM Plex Sans + IBM Plex Sans Arabic | Bilingual UI, equal weight |

Arabic reads right-to-left. Keep the Arabic line optically centered under Latin; do not flip letterforms.

## Clear space and size

- Clear space around any lockup = the height of the Arabic *alif* in that lockup.  
- Minimum digital width for the full stacked lockup: 160 px. Below that, use the seal only.  
- Minimum print: 18 mm wide for the stacked lockup; 8 mm diameter for the seal.

---

## Do / do not

**Do**
- Keep the gold crest on night as the default mark.
- Pair Latin and Arabic as equals, not as a caption.
- Use gold only in the crest, type, and jewellery finish — not as a cheap bottle shell.

**Do not**
- Stretch Arabic, add a giant standalone `M`, or set `PERFUMES AND COSMETICS` larger than the founder line.
- Sell a product whose only name is “Marahil” (see naming file). The house is MARAHIL; juices have chapter names.
- Put a second invented seal (MRK five-arc pewter disc) beside this crest.
