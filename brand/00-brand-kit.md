# MARAHIL Brand Kit

**House:** MARAHIL  
**Arabic:** مراحل  
**Legal lockup:** MARAHIL by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics  
**Status:** Concept kit (Phase 1). Not production vectors yet.

Primary system: **Direction A wordmark + Direction B seal**.  
Direction C (founder signature) is a supporting line for press and packaging credits, not the main logo.

### Master hardware (do not redesign per SKU)

One cap / lid / box medallion for the whole house. Production traces this frame:

- Master seal: [visuals/marahil-cons-cap-top.png](visuals/marahil-cons-cap-top.png)  
  Circular brushed pewter disc. `MARAHIL` arched at the top. Serif `MRK` in the center. Two concentric rings. Nothing else.

**Same disc on:** bottle cap top, box lid, cream lid, candle lid, attar cap, discovery vial caps, jewelry pendants.  
**Same wordmark on:** box face — stacked `MARAHIL` + `مراحل`.

Juice name (ISHRAQ, MARIA, ATHAR…) lives only on the **plaque or side print**, never inside the seal.  
Never swap in a hexagonal cap, a letter-M crest, or yellow gold.

---

## Palette

Use these four colors only. Do not introduce bright gold, rose-gold gradients, or juice-colored logos.

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| **Ink** | `#1A1614` | Wordmark, Arabic mark, body text on ivory |
| **Ivory** | `#F4EFE6` | Paper, boxes, site background, reversed type |
| **Pewter** | `#8A8580` | Seal metal, rules, founder-line color on ink |
| **Stage red** | `#6B2A28` | Inner ring of the seal only. Never a fill, never a website background |

**Rules**
- One metal: pewter. Recoloring the seal to yellow gold makes the house look like a replica stall.
- No gradients on the logo.
- Juice color lives on the **bottle collar**, not in the logo.

---

## Typography

| Role | Spec | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Latin display | High-contrast serif, all caps, tracked | `MARAHIL` only. Do not set `PERFUMES AND COSMETICS` at the same size as the house name. |
| Arabic display | Modern Naskh (or refined Diwani for ceremony) | `مراحل`. Never mechanically squash Arabic to match Latin width. |
| Founder line | Small-caps grotesque, letterspaced | `by Maria Raheel Khan Perfumes and Cosmetics` |
| UI / later site | Clean grotesque (e.g. similar to Neue Haas / Inter) | Not part of the logo |

Arabic reads right-to-left. Keep the Arabic line optically centered under Latin; do not flip letterforms.

## Clear space and size

- Clear space around any lockup = the height of the Arabic *alif* in that lockup.  
- Minimum digital width for the full stacked lockup: 160 px. Below that, use the seal only.  
- Minimum print: 18 mm wide for the stacked lockup; 8 mm diameter for the seal.

---

## Do / do not

**Do**
- Keep ink on ivory as the default.
- Pair Latin and Arabic as equals, not as a caption.
- Use pewter for metal, stage red only as a hairline.

**Do not**
- Outline, drop-shadow, bevel, or put the mark on busy photography without an ivory or ink plate.
- Stretch Arabic, add a giant standalone `M`, or set `PERFUMES AND COSMETICS` larger than the founder line.
- Sell a product whose only name is “Marahil” (see naming file). The house is MARAHIL; juices have chapter names.
