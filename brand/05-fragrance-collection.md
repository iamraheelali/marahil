# Fragrance collection

**Thesis:** five stages of becoming, one founder signature, plus a short **pale-woods** chapter of three juices (high-end, linear, quiet).  
These are **briefs for a perfumer**, not finished formulas. All unisex in copy. No “for him / for her” at launch.

**Avoid cloning** Ard Al Zaafaran’s existing Marahil (lemon, oud, saffron, musk). Ishraq may use saffron; it must not smell like that SKU.

The pale-woods three occupy creamy sandalwood, cool cypress, and dry rosewood. Write them as original MARAHIL juices. Do not name other houses in copy, ads, or PDPs.

**Concentrations:** EDP for the five and the woods three; extrait for Maria.  
**Launch price:** AED 480–980 for the five; **AED 780–1,080** for the pale-woods three; Maria higher; discovery set as entry.

## 1. Bad’ — بدء — The beginning

**Role:** opening, day, first spray of the house.  
**Family:** citrus aromatic / pale woods.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Bergamot, green mandarin, a pinch of black pepper |
| Heart | Neroli, green tea, petitgrain |
| Base | Pale cedar, musks, a dry white wood |

**Wearer:** morning, office, heat.  
**Layer with:** Athar (skin) or Ishraq (more rose).  
**Do not:** sweet cola citrus, loud aquatic, “fresh” detergent musk.

---

## 2. Ishraq — إشراق — Radiance

**Role:** light, saffron-rose without tourist oud.  
**Family:** floral spicy / soft oud.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Pink pepper, saffron, a drop of bitter orange |
| Heart | Damask rose, orris hint, tea rose |
| Base | Soft oud, benzoin, clean amber |

**Wearer:** day-to-evening, gifts, Ramadan nights that still feel luminous.  
**Layer with:** Wasl (deeper) or Athar (softer).  
**Do not:** syrupy rose-oud, lemon-oud opening that reads as the Ard Al Zaafaran juice.

---

## 3. Wasl — وصل — The meeting

**Role:** the heart of the house; where two stages meet.  
**Family:** amber floral / woody.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Cardamom, bergamot peel |
| Heart | Orris, rose, sandalwood |
| Base | Amber, a restrained oud, tonka (dry, not cake) |

**Wearer:** the bottle you finish.  
**Layer with:** Layl (night) or Ishraq (brighter).  
**Do not:** heavy gourmand, mosque-incense overload (that is Layl’s job).

---

## 4. Layl — ليل — Night

**Role:** after dark, incense, leather.  
**Family:** oriental leather / incense.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Elemi, black pepper |
| Heart | Incense, rose absolute (dry), leather |
| Base | Labdanum, musk, a smear of birch or cade (careful, IFRA) |

**Wearer:** evening, winter AC, bakhoor-adjacent but wearable.  
**Layer with:** Wasl or Maria.  
**Do not:** campfire smoke, cheap “oud bomb.”

---

## 5. Athar — أثر — Afterglow

**Role:** skin scent; the house “your skin but better.”  
**Family:** musky woody / soft gourmand.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Barely there: pear skin or tea |
| Heart | Sandalwood, iris, a veil of orange blossom |
| Base | Clean musk, vanilla (dry), cashmeran-type softness |

**Wearer:** daily, layering base, the ritual line (hair/body) starts here.  
**Layer with:** everything.  
**Do not:** baby-powder musk, loud vanilla bakery.

## 6. Maria — ماريا — Signature extrait

**Role:** founder juice; the bottle people gift and remember.  
**Family:** richer Ishraq × Wasl.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Saffron, pink pepper, a flash of bergamot |
| Heart | Rose, orris, a personal “skin” accord |
| Base | Sandalwood, amber, soft oud, a dry vanilla-musk trail |

**Wearer:** signature, evening, the House page hero.  
**Layer with:** Athar hair mist / body oil (Phase 1 beauty).  
**Do not:** a celebrity-name juice that smells unrelated to the five stages.

## Pale woods (three juices)

Quiet, expensive, almost linear. Creamy sandalwood and dry pale woods — not oud, not gourmand, not a tourist incense. Built for AC interiors and close conversation. Same bottle as the rest of the house; collar in sand / sage-pewter / warm wood.

### 7. Hadu’ — هدوء — Stillness

**Role:** the house’s high sandalwood; skin, cream, almost motionless.  
**Family:** woody / sandalwood / musk.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | A cool lift: cypress needle, a drop of myrtle, almost no citrus |
| Heart | Creamy sandalwood (Mysore-quality brief), a pale rose that is wood not bouquet |
| Base | Sandalwood again, white musk, a dry milky wood, the faintest amber |

**Structure:** low contrast. The opening is already the drydown. Projection is a halo, then a scarf of wood on skin for 8–10h in AC.  
**Wearer:** the person who wants one expensive quiet spray, not a statement oud.  
**Layer with:** Athar (softer) or Sarw (greener).  
**Do not:** sweet coconut wood, loud sawdust, copious vanilla, or a fruit opening.

---

### 8. Sarw — سرو — Cypress

**Role:** the cool green wood; shade, resin, dry air.  
**Family:** aromatic woody.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Italian cypress, crushed myrtle, a green pepper dust |
| Heart | Cedar, a dry rose-wood, faint incense smoke (clean, not church) |
| Base | Pale sandalwood, vetiver (short, not dirt), musk |

**Wear:** 6–8h; more lift than Hadu’ in the first hour, then the same quiet wood.  
**Wearer:** heat, linen, day. The “I walked through a grove” juice.  
**Layer with:** Hadu’ (cream on the cypress) or Bad’ (brighter).  
**Do not:** pine-cleaner, aftershave fougère, aquatic.

---

### 9. Ghusn — غصن — The bough

**Role:** warmer pale wood; rosewood, dry amber, still not sweet.  
**Family:** woody amber / rosewood.

| | Notes |
|---|---|
| Top | Brazilian-style rosewood (legal substitutes), a strip of bergamot peel |
| Heart | Sandalwood, a honeyed wood without dessert, orris dust |
| Base | Dry amber, musk, a polished furniture-wood trail |

**Wear:** 8–10h; the richest of the three, still close. Evening wood without leather or oud.  
**Wearer:** the gift for someone who already owns Ishraq and wants woods instead of rose.  
**Layer with:** Hadu’ or Wasl.  
**Do not:** caramel wood, loud ambergris funk, oud.
