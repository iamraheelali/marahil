---

## How to read a profile

- **Family / concentration / sizes** as a perfumer would brief a lab  
- **Pyramid** is the intended structure, not a GC-MS  
- **Wear:** longevity and projection for UAE heat (AC interiors vs outdoor)  
- **IFRA:** keep oakmoss, citrus, and rose oxides in legal bands; bakhoor is incense, not skin
