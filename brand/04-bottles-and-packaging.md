# Bottles and packaging

The bottle is the second logo. One silhouette for the whole launch line. Color lives on the **collar**, not in a new shape per SKU.

Uses Phase 1 kit: ink, ivory, pewter, stage red. Seal on the cap. Wordmark on the box.

Concept frames: [visuals/marahil-bottle-ishraq.png](visuals/marahil-bottle-ishraq.png) (hero) and [visuals/marahil-bottle-lineup.png](visuals/marahil-bottle-lineup.png) (one silhouette, collar changes). These are direction, not tooling drawings.

---

## Hero bottle

- Heavy glass, architectural, **five facets or five engraved rings** (the stages)
- Magnetic pewter cap with the **MRK seal** on top
- Collar: colored enamel or lacquer identifying the *marhala*
- Plaque: Latin juice name (e.g. ISHRAQ)
- Collar or base: Arabic house mark `مراحل`
- Atomizer: quality crimp, not a cheap screw sprayer
- Sizes: **50ml** and **100ml** EDP; **10ml** travel in the same silhouette scaled; **2ml** vials for discovery

**Do not:** a unique bottle per juice at launch, replica-style jewel caps, loud gold, or printed plastic sleeves.

---

## Collar colors (marhala coding)

| Juice | Collar | Glass / juice |
|---|---|---|
| Bad’ | Pale pewter | Almost clear, green-gold hint |
| Ishraq | Warm rose-metal (not pink plastic) | Soft amber-rose |
| Wasl | Deep pewter | Golden amber |
| Layl | Ink | Dark, almost opaque |
| Athar | Ivory enamel | Skin-pale |
| Maria (extrait) | Stage red hairline on pewter | Deeper, more viscous look |

The logo itself stays ink/ivory/pewter. Only the collar changes.

---

## Box and unboxing

- Rigid board, ivory exterior, ink interior (or the reverse for Layl / Maria)
- Debossed stacked wordmark on the lid
- Optional wax or sticker of the MRK seal
- Inner tray holding the bottle; bilingual leaflet (English left / Arabic right, or a mirrored RTL edition)
- Ribbon: pewter or ivory, not satin gold

Discovery set: ivory sleeve, five (or six) vials in a row like chapters, titled **The First Marahil**.
