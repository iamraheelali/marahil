# Bottles and packaging

The bottle is the second logo. One silhouette for the whole launch line. Color lives on the **collar**, not in a new shape per SKU.

Uses the gold house crest on a matte-black cap. Juice name on the plaque. Collar colour still codes the *marhala*.

Concept frames: [visuals/marahil-cons-bottle-ishraq.png](visuals/marahil-cons-bottle-ishraq.png) (hero) and [visuals/marahil-cons-lineup.png](visuals/marahil-cons-lineup.png) (one silhouette, collar changes).

---

## Hero bottle

- Heavy glass, architectural, **five facets or five engraved rings** (the stages)
- Magnetic **matte-black cap** with the **gold MARAHIL crest** on top
- Collar: colored enamel or lacquer identifying the *marhala*
- Plaque: Latin juice name (e.g. ISHRAQ)
- Collar or base: Arabic house mark `مراحل`
- Atomizer: quality crimp, not a cheap screw sprayer
- Sizes: **50ml** and **100ml** EDP; **10ml** travel in the same silhouette scaled; **2ml** vials for discovery

**Do not:** a unique bottle per juice at launch, replica-style jewel caps, a yellow-gold bottle shell, or printed plastic sleeves. Gold belongs in the crest.

## Collar colors (marhala coding)

| Juice | Collar | Glass / juice |
|---|---|---|
| Bad’ | Pale pewter | Almost clear, green-gold hint |
| Ishraq | Warm rose-metal (not pink plastic) | Soft amber-rose |
| Wasl | Deep pewter | Golden amber |
| Layl | Ink | Dark, almost opaque |
| Athar | Ivory enamel | Skin-pale |
| Maria (extrait) | Stage red hairline on pewter | Deeper, more viscous look |

The logo itself stays the gold crest. Only the collar changes.

## Box and unboxing

- Two-piece **rigid hard-paper** setup box (greyboard wrapped in matte night paper) — haute, not lacquered jewellery wood and not cheap folding carton
- Gold foil crest hot-stamped on the lid; juice name on a side plaque in English and Arabic, never inside the crest
- Tagline on the box: **Every stage has a scent.**
- House line on the base: **MRK Perfumes and Cosmetics · Abu Dhabi, UAE**
- Simple black paperboard tray; bilingual leaflet
- Simple black paperboard tray; bilingual leaflet
- Photograph on night `#100E0C` or ivory paper — same field as the site, never a colder pure black

Discovery set: black sleeve, six vials in a row like chapters, titled **The First Stages**.
